Decisional Balance Matrix

A structured way to think through a change you are considering — not to find the "right" answer, but to see the full picture of what you are actually weighing.

what decision or change are you weighing?
How to use this. Fill in each quadrant as honestly as you can — including the benefits of not changing, which are real and worth naming. Ambivalence is not a problem to solve. It is information. When you are done, use the reflection space to notice what stands out.
