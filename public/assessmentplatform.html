Assessment Introduction

Before we begin, I want to explain what you can expect today.

I will be asking you a number of personal questions. Please answer as openly and accurately as you can. You do not need to be certain about every answer, and it is okay to ask for clarification. The more complete the information you provide, the more accurate and useful the final report is likely to be.

Some parts of the assessment may feel uncomfortable because of the topics we discuss and because the process requires sustained concentration over several hours. We have scheduled breaks, but please let me know at any point if you need an additional break.

You are welcome to drink water, eat snacks, change positions, stand, or use a fidget during the assessment. Please do what helps you feel comfortable and able to participate, within reasonable limits.

The assessment will take approximately four hours. Your report will be returned within seven business days. It will include a diagnostic evaluation focused on ADHD and autism symptoms. It will also consider whether other conditions may better explain your symptoms or may be occurring alongside them.

This is a diagnostic evaluation, not a comprehensive neuropsychological assessment.

Do you have any questions before we begin?
