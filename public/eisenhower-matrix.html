Eisenhower Matrix

A three-step tool for getting everything out of your head, sorting what actually matters, and exporting your tasks straight to Google Tasks.

brain dump
categorize
export

Step 1: brain dump

Write down everything on your mind — tasks, worries, errands, things you keep forgetting. Don't filter or judge. One item per line.

No order required. No format required. Just write until your head feels emptier.

Break tasks into their smallest real steps

Vague tasks like "Amazon return" never get done because your brain doesn't know where to start. List each physical step separately — or use the subtask feature in step two to break them down there.

example
Amazon return
place return request on Amazon
print return code
take package to Amazon drop box

Step 2: sort your tasks

Click a task to select it, then click a quadrant to place it. Use the move icon to re-categorize, and the subtask icon to break a task into steps.

          Step 3: export

          Export your sorted tasks to Google Tasks, or download as a CSV to print or save.

          How to import into Google Tasks
          1
          Click export to Google Tasks above. A file called eisenhower-tasks.csv will download to your device.
          2
          Open Google Tasks in your browser or the Gmail sidebar. Click the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top right corner of the Tasks panel.
          3
          Select Import tasks, then choose the CSV file you just downloaded.
          4
          Your tasks will appear in Google Tasks with their quadrant label in the notes field. Subtasks are exported as individual items with their parent task noted.

          Import is available on desktop browsers only — the Google Tasks mobile app does not support CSV import. Tasks will appear in your default list and can be reorganized once imported.
